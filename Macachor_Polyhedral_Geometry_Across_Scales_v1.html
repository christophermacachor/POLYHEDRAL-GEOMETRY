Arctic summer-solstice test

The June solstice geometry produces polar day north of the Arctic Circle. Daylight duration depends on latitude and solar declination.

H₀ = arccos(−tan φ tan δ)
If −tan φ tan δ ≤ −1, continuous daylight; if ≥ 1, polar night.

Evidence boundary

Observed: polar-day duration, solar declination, station temperatures and Earth–Sun distance.

Model representation: polyhedral face geometry and face-resolved projected area.

Not established by this visualization: that the physical Earth is a regular polyhedron, or that a planar face causes the Earth–Sun distance or the midnight Sun.
